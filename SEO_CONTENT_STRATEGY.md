# ShortClipr SEO + AEO Content System

## Goal

Make ShortClipr the clearest, most useful source for creators who want to turn an idea into an animated, faceless, educational, story, quiz, or short-form social video.

The objective is not to publish the most pages. It is to make every indexed URL satisfy one recognizable user intent better than a generic AI summary: answer the question quickly, teach a usable method, show an original example, explain limitations honestly, and offer the next useful action.

## What “best” means for this site

1. **Intent ownership** — every URL has one primary user job and natural query variants. Product pages target maker/tool intent; guides target “how do I…” intent; idea pages target discovery intent.
2. **Answer-first structure** — the first meaningful section answers the title question in plain language. Definitions, steps, examples, and caveats use descriptive headings and short paragraphs.
3. **Original utility** — every article includes a framework, decision rule, checklist, example brief, or review test that a creator can use immediately.
4. **Trust by design** — capabilities, concepts, examples, and finished work are clearly distinguished. Factual claims are conservative and include verification guidance.
5. **Topical architecture** — commercial pages link to the guide that helps the visitor succeed; guides link back to the most relevant product page; hub pages organize the cluster.
6. **AEO readability** — direct answers, question-shaped headings, short self-contained passages, numbered steps, and explicit entities make pages easy to quote accurately.
7. **Conversion without hype** — the CTA follows the reader’s job: choose a format, write a brief, or open the creation desk. No unsupported promises about virality or ranking.

## Page jobs

| Page family | Primary job | Required content |
|---|---|---|
| Product/solution page | Help a ready-to-create visitor choose a format | Definition, who it is for, workflow, example brief, limitations, related guide, CTA |
| How-to guide/article | Teach a task from start to finish | Direct answer, numbered method, worked example, review checklist, product bridge |
| Ideas hub | Help a visitor choose a topic or format | Categorized ideas, selection rubric, examples, links into clusters |
| Guides hub | Establish editorial depth and navigation | Clear categories, concise descriptions, update context |
| About/editorial policy | Establish trust and provenance | Who writes, what is verified, what is conceptual, how updates work |

## Editorial rules

- Use the words a creator would search for, but write for the creator rather than a crawler.
- Put the primary concept in the title, H1, opening answer, and one useful internal link—not in every sentence.
- Prefer specific verbs and concrete examples over hype adjectives.
- Never imply that ShortClipr publishes to TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube automatically unless the product actually does so.
- Never present concept artwork as customer output, a benchmark, testimonial, or guaranteed result.
- Do not invent statistics, customer stories, rankings, integrations, or platform requirements.
- Use visible publication and update dates only when the content was materially reviewed.
- Make each important paragraph self-contained enough to be quoted without losing context.

## Measurement loop

Track by URL and intent: indexed/canonical coverage, impressions, clicks, CTR, conversions to the creation desk, assisted conversions from guides, AI citations/grounding queries where available, and pages with impressions but weak CTR. Refresh when the query intent, product capability, factual claim, or platform guidance changes—not merely to change the date.
