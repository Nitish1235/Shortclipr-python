What is an animated YouTube Shorts maker?

An animated YouTube Short is a vertical video that explains, compares or tells a compact story through visuals and narration. Start with one self-contained idea and give it an opening, a visible development and a satisfying ending.

THE STARTING POINT

Animated YouTube Shorts maker.

How do you create an animated YouTube Short?

Choose one clear idea, write a brief describing the audience and visual story, and select a vertical format. ShortClipr lets you explore formats including explainers, stories and quizzes.

Three Shorts ideas

Explain why the moon can appear in daylight; follow a trade route on a map; or tell a fable about a lamp helping a plant. Each idea has one clear question or change.

Review before uploading

Watch the finished video on a phone, check factual claims and make sure the opening makes sense without prior context. Upload the result to your channel yourself.
