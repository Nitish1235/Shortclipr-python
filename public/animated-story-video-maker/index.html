QUICK ANSWER

What is an animated story video maker?

An animated story video gives a character a goal, introduces one meaningful obstacle and ends with a visible change. ShortClipr is designed for compact stories and fables that can be understood as a complete short.

THE STARTING POINT

Animated story video maker.

Use a three-beat spine: the character wants something, one obstacle forces a choice, and the final action shows what changed.

Try a creative brief

“A paper boat fears the rain until it discovers a puddle can carry it home. Give it one obstacle, one brave choice, and a hopeful final image.”

Check that the ending pays off the opening and that each scene advances the character’s goal.
