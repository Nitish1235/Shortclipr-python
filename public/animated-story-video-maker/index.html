QUICK ANSWER

What is an animated story video maker?

An animated story video gives a character a goal, introduces one meaningful obstacle and ends with a visible change. ShortClipr is designed for compact stories and fables that can be understood as a complete short.

THE STARTING POINT

Animated story video maker.

Give your character a simple goal, put one obstacle in the way, then show what changes. In a short film, a single meaningful action can carry the story.

Try a creative brief

“A paper boat is afraid of the rain until it discovers the puddle can carry it home. Tell a gentle story with a hopeful ending.”

Focus on one character and one change. Too many locations or events can make a short story harder to follow.
