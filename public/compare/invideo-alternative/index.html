Where ShortClipr fits

ShortClipr is designed for creators who want to turn a written idea or prompt into a finished animated short. Documented creative formats include explainers, comparisons, quizzes, character stories, mystery, maps, what-if simulations, myth-busting and more.

Paid plans support 15-, 30-, 45- and 60-second shorts in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 formats. ShortClipr does not claim automatic social publishing: you review and download the finished video, then choose where to publish it.
