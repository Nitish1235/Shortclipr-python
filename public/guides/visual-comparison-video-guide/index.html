How to make a visual comparison video

Compare two things on the same basis: the same unit, time period or condition. State what is being compared before presenting the difference.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Choose a meaningful comparison

Compare two things on the same basis: the same unit, time period or condition. State what is being compared before presenting the difference.

Give the viewer context

A number is easier to understand when the viewer knows the scale. If you compare distances, use the same units and make clear whether the figures are approximate.

Try a brief

Create a short visual comparison of light and sound. Explain that light travels much faster, use a storm as the example and end with why thunder arrives after a lightning flash.

Review the claim

Check figures and labels against reliable sources. ShortClipr’s comparisons and visual facts format can help frame the idea; you remain responsible for the accuracy of the finished video.
