How to make animated TikTok videos

Write the thing your viewer will learn, feel or discover. Choose an opening that introduces it immediately.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Start with the point

Write the thing your viewer will learn, feel or discover. Choose an opening that introduces it immediately.

Make the action visible

A moving object, a question, or a before-and-after sequence can carry the idea. Avoid placing essential detail at the edge of the frame.

Describe your direction

Include the subject, audience, mood and outcome in your brief. Example: explain a common coffee myth with a playful comparison and a clear answer.

Check your finished video

Watch at phone size and verify any claims. Add your own caption or post details when uploading if they are useful to your audience.
