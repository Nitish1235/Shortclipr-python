How to tell a story in under a minute

Introduce a character and what they want in a way the viewer can understand quickly.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Give someone a goal

Introduce a character and what they want in a way the viewer can understand quickly.

Add one obstacle

Choose a problem that creates a decision. A tiny obstacle can be compelling when the audience cares about the outcome.

Make the ending change something

The character can succeed, fail or see the situation differently. Give the ending a visual action that pays off the beginning.

Remove the extra plot

If a scene does not support the goal, obstacle or ending, consider leaving it for another story.
