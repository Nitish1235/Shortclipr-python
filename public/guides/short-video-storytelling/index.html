How to tell a story in under a minute

Use a simple story spine instead of a crowded plot. Let the audience understand what the character wants through an action.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Give someone a goal

Let the audience understand what the character wants through an action.

Add one obstacle

Choose a problem that forces a decision.

Make the choice visible

Show the character trying, changing direction, or discovering something.

End with change

Use a final image that echoes or reverses the opening.

Cut the side plot

If a scene does not support the goal, obstacle, choice, or ending, save it.
