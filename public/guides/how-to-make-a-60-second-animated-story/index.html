How to Make a 60-Second Animated Story.

A compact story framework for a one-minute animated short with one character, one obstacle and a satisfying change.

ShortClipr Editorial Team · November 21, 2026

QUICK ANSWER

Build a short around setup, obstacle, choice and payoff. Give one character a visible goal, make the obstacle understandable, and reserve time for the ending to land.

Start with one clear outcome

A useful short has one audience and one result. Narrow the subject until you can explain what viewers should understand or feel after watching.

Avoid broad topics and unrelated facts. One focused outcome makes the story, visuals and review process easier.

Choose a format that serves the idea

Use a diagram for a process, a map for a journey, a timeline for sequence, a comparison for differences, a quiz for a fair question, or a character story for an emotional change.

The format should help the viewer understand the subject, not simply decorate it.

Write a production-ready brief

Describe the audience, subject, visible action, tone, runtime, constraints and ending. Use specific verbs and observable details instead of vague adjectives.

For factual topics, verify claims and provide reliable source notes. For recurring characters, keep a stable reference description.

Review before publishing

Check the opening, scene logic, narration, factual accuracy, captions, pronunciation, continuity, pacing and final takeaway. Watch the result on a phone-sized screen.
