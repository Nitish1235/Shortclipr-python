How to make an animated explainer video

Turn one difficult question into a clear visual lesson. Write the exact question your viewer needs answered. If it needs a lecture, narrow the topic.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Start with one answerable question

Write the exact question your viewer needs answered. If it needs a lecture, narrow the topic.

Define the learner and takeaway

Complete: “After watching, a beginner should be able to explain…” This prevents a pile of facts.

Choose a visual relationship

Use a comparison, sequence, map, diagram, or transformation that makes the idea visible.

Write opening, middle, and close

Open with the question, show the relationship, then repeat the takeaway in plain language.

Review before publishing

Check facts, labels, narration, contrast, and phone readability.
