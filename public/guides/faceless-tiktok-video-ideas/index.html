Faceless TikTok video ideas

Show an unexpected comparison: how large is a blue whale beside a bus? Give context rather than presenting a number alone.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Try a visual fact

Show an unexpected comparison: how large is a blue whale beside a bus? Give context rather than presenting a number alone.

Tell a tiny story

A character wants something, an obstacle appears and a choice changes the ending. Try a lost key, an impatient robot or a plant reaching for light.

Invite a guess

Ask a question, pause to let viewers think and reveal an answer with a brief explanation. Check the answer before publishing.

Build a series

Use a consistent subject or question format across several posts, while giving every video a distinct idea. Create for the audience rather than promising a particular result.

Five prompts to make your own

Explain an everyday science question; follow a historical journey on a map; show a visual comparison; introduce a mystery clue; or ask a quiz question with a satisfying reveal. In ShortClipr, select the format that best fits your idea and describe your intended audience.
