How to make faceless YouTube Shorts

Plan repeatable Shorts without appearing on camera. Pick a topic and recognizable format: one question, historical moment, comparison, or puzzle.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Choose a series promise

Pick a topic and recognizable format: one question, historical moment, comparison, or puzzle.

Give every Short one job

Answer one question, tell one compact story, or reveal one useful comparison.

Write a visual brief

Name audience, opening, scenes, tone, narration, and ending. Say what should be visible.

Review for discovery

Watch without prior context and check the first seconds, phone readability, and payoff.

Publish responsibly

Verify claims, rights, captions, and current YouTube requirements.
