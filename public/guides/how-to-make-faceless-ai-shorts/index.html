How to make faceless AI Shorts

Use AI to develop a short while keeping the creative brief and human review in control. Name the audience, question, opening visual, narration tone, and ending. “Make it engaging” is not enough direction.

Using AI for faceless Shorts works best as a creative partnership: you supply the editorial direction and fact-checking; the tool handles assembly.

Start with a specific brief

Name the audience, question, opening visual, narration tone, and ending. “Make it engaging” is not enough direction.

Choose a repeatable format

Use an explainer, comparison, quiz, mystery, or tiny story so the system has a clear structure to develop.

Review the generated interpretation

Check facts, visual meaning, pronunciation, pacing, captions, and whether the result actually answers the brief.

Add your editorial judgment

Rewrite generic lines, remove unsupported claims, and keep only visuals that serve the idea.

Publish with care

Check rights, disclosures, captions, and current platform rules before uploading.
