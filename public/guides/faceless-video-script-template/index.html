Faceless video script template

A simple script structure for animated Shorts with narration and visuals. State the question, conflict, or surprising observation in one clear line.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Hook

State the question, conflict, or surprising observation in one clear line.

Visual proof

Describe what the audience should see while the narration explains the relationship or action.

Payoff

Answer the question, complete the story, or reveal the clue.

Takeaway

Give one sentence the viewer can remember or use.

Review

Read the script aloud, check every claim, and make sure each visual adds information rather than repeating the narration.
