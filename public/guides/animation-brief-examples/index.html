Animated video brief examples

Practical briefs for explainers, history, stories, quizzes, and visual facts. Explain why the Moon can be visible during the day to a beginner; show the Earth, Moon, and daylight; end with one verified takeaway.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Explainer brief

Explain why the Moon can be visible during the day to a beginner; show the Earth, Moon, and daylight; end with one verified takeaway.

History brief

Follow a trade route on a map; introduce places, show what moved, and explain why it mattered; verify the details.

Story brief

A lamp helps a seedling through a rainy night; give the lamp a goal, obstacle, and hopeful action.

Quiz brief

Ask whether light or sound travels faster; give time to guess, reveal, and explain the lightning example.

Visual-fact brief

Compare two familiar objects using one unit and a clear scale; state what the comparison does and does not prove.
