Vertical, square, or widescreen video: how to choose

Choose the frame from the destination and the visual idea. A 9:16 frame gives a tall canvas; keep labels and action in a safe area.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Choose vertical for phone-first shorts

A 9:16 frame gives a tall canvas; keep labels and action in a safe area.

Choose square for balanced compositions

A 1:1 frame works when the subject is central and portable across placements.

Choose widescreen for horizontal relationships

A 16:9 frame gives room for maps, comparisons, presentations, and wide scenes.

Design for the crop

Keep essential information away from edges and preview at actual size.

Check current platform rules

Specifications and layouts change; confirm before publishing.
