How to make faceless educational videos

Choose something your audience should understand after watching. “Understand why the moon is visible in daylight” is more useful than “learn astronomy.”

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Start with one learning goal

Choose something your audience should understand after watching. “Understand why the moon is visible in daylight” is more useful than “learn astronomy.”

Choose the visual explanation

Show the objects and their relationship in a simple sequence. Introduce unfamiliar terms only when they help the viewer follow the explanation.

Try this brief

Explain why the moon can be visible during the day to a curious beginner. Use a clear visual explanation, a friendly voice and one takeaway. Keep the video vertical and approximately 30 seconds.

Create and review

In ShortClipr, explore the animated explainer format and choose your frame, voice and duration. Check the narration and visuals against reliable sources before sharing.
