How to make faceless educational videos

Teach a focused lesson with animation and narration instead of a presenter. Use “After watching, the learner can…” and keep it observable.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Write one learning objective

Use “After watching, the learner can…” and keep it observable.

Choose the example

A familiar object, process, or comparison can carry the explanation.

Sequence the explanation

Introduce parts, show their relationship, then restate the idea.

Design for access

Use readable text, clear narration, contrast, and appropriate pacing.

Review the evidence

Check sources and terminology, especially for high-stakes subjects.
