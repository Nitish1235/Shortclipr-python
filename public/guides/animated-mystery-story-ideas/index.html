How to make an animated mystery short

A clock stops whenever someone enters a room. A red umbrella appears at the wrong door. Pick one detail that invites an obvious question.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Begin with something unusual

A clock stops whenever someone enters a room. A red umbrella appears at the wrong door. Pick one detail that invites an obvious question.

Make the clue visible

Let the audience see the relevant object or change. The mystery should work even if the viewer has never seen a previous episode.

Use a compact story brief

A librarian notices a book has moved overnight. Show two clues, build suspense and reveal that a curious cat has been rearranging the shelf. Keep it playful rather than frightening.

Choose the direction

In ShortClipr, explore mystery and suspense, select a vertical frame for a phone-first short and review whether the ending follows the clues.
