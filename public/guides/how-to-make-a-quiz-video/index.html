How to make a quiz video

Make a quiz clear, fair, readable, and useful. Avoid ambiguous wording and arbitrary trick answers.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Choose a verifiable question

Avoid ambiguous wording and arbitrary trick answers.

Show the choices

Make question and options readable without pausing.

Create a thinking pause

Give enough time to form a guess; complex questions need context too.

Reveal and explain

State the answer, then give the shortest reason that makes it memorable.

Test the experience

Check contrast, sound, answer accuracy, and unsupported interaction claims.
