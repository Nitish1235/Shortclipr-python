How to make a short animated history video

Instead of covering an entire civilization, pick one event, invention or journey. Identify the person or change the audience should follow.

A useful short starts with a clear purpose. These steps will help you shape an idea for your audience and review it before sharing.

Choose a small story

Instead of covering an entire civilization, pick one event, invention or journey. Identify the person or change the audience should follow.

Build a three-part timeline

Set the place and period, show the turning point, then explain why it mattered. Avoid suggesting that a simplified account tells the whole story.

Write your brief

Create a short animated history video about the first practical use of the printing press in Europe. Introduce the problem of copying texts, explain the change and finish with its broader impact.

Verify before publishing

Check dates, names, locations and claims against reputable historical sources. Use ShortClipr’s historical story or maps direction as a starting point, then review the result carefully.
