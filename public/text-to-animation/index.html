QUICK ANSWER

What does text-to-animation mean?

Text-to-animation turns a written topic, question or creative brief into a visual short. ShortClipr is designed to help you describe the subject, audience, tone and ending, then explore that idea as a finished animated video rather than a timeline you have to assemble.

THE STARTING POINT

Turn text into an animated short.

Write four things first: who is watching, what they should understand or feel, what should be visible, and what changes by the end.

Try a creative brief

“A small lamp helps a seedling through a rainy night. Create a warm, hopeful short with one obstacle, one visible act of help, and a quiet ending.”

Review the output as an interpretation of your brief. Correct unclear copy, missing context, and unsupported claims before sharing.
