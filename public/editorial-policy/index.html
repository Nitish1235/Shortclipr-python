How ShortClipr creates, reviews and updates its public guides, examples and product information.

OUR STANDARD

How is ShortClipr content reviewed?

The ShortClipr Editorial Team writes for creators first: answer the question clearly, distinguish concepts from finished work, avoid unsupported performance claims, and review factual or platform-specific details before publication.

01

Clarity

Every guide should answer a specific creator question in plain language. Important limitations and next steps should be visible rather than hidden behind promotional wording.

02

Accuracy

Factual claims are checked before publication. Platform requirements can change, so creators are directed to review current official requirements before uploading.

03

Originality

Briefs, examples and creative frameworks should add practical value instead of repeating generic summaries. Illustrative concepts are labelled so they are not mistaken for finished customer work.

04

Updates

Guides display publication and update dates. Material corrections and meaningful changes should update the visible date and the structured page information together.

05

Commercial transparency

ShortClipr content can explain how the platform supports a task, but it must not invent testimonials, rankings, customer results or guarantees. Product availability is described as it exists.
