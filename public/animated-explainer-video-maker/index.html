QUICK ANSWER

What is an animated explainer video maker?

An animated explainer video makes one focused idea easier to understand through a short visual sequence and narration. With ShortClipr, you begin with the question, audience and takeaway, then shape them into an animated short you can review before sharing.

THE STARTING POINT

Animated explainer video maker.

Start with a question your audience already has. Explain the answer in one sentence, then choose a visual analogy that makes the relationship easy to follow.

Try a creative brief

“Explain why the moon can be visible during the day to a curious beginner. Use a calm, playful tone and end with one takeaway.”

A strong explainer makes one promise, shows the answer through a sequence, and leaves the viewer with something they can repeat. Check factual claims before sharing.

What is an animated explainer video?

An animated explainer uses moving visuals and narration to make an idea easier to understand. A short explainer works best when it answers one specific question for one audience.

From question to finished short

Start with your topic in ShortClipr, choose Animated explainer, then select your voice, visual direction, duration and frame. Review the result and verify any factual claims before sharing.

Three directions to try

Explain an everyday question, compare two related concepts, or show the steps in a process. For example: why does the moon appear in daylight? How does a queue become congested? What happens when a seed germinates?
