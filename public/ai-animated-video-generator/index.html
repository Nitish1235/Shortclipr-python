DIRECT ANSWER

What is an AI animated video generator?

An AI animated video generator converts a written topic, story outline, or creative brief into a multi-scene animated video with narration, pacing, and visual transitions. Unlike raw clip generators that output silent 5-second shots, ShortClipr assembles a complete 15- to 60-second short you can review and download.

WORKFLOW & FIT

Complete shorts instead of raw fragments.

Many generative video tools output disconnected 4-second clips that still require an external editor, voiceover tool, and script assembly. ShortClipr treats your brief as one cohesive film—planning scenes, matching visual metaphors, and timing narration from opening hook to final payoff.

Worked brief example

“Create a 45-second vertical explainer showing why airplane cabin windows have a tiny breather hole. Start with a passenger noticing the hole, zoom into the three-pane pressure layers, and end with one clear safety takeaway.”

ShortClipr is not a long-form YouTube editor or podcast clipper. It is purpose-built for original 15- to 60-second animated shorts where visual clarity matters most.
