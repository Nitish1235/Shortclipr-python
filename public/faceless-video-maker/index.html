QUICK ANSWER

What is a faceless video maker?

A faceless video communicates through animation, objects, characters, text and narration instead of footage of the creator. ShortClipr helps creators plan explainers, stories, quizzes and visual facts without needing to record themselves on camera.

THE STARTING POINT

Faceless video maker for curious creators.

Choose a subject you can return to, such as strange history or everyday science. Give each short one focused question or story rather than trying to cover everything at once.

Try a creative brief

“Create a faceless short explaining why octopuses change color. Make it curious and accessible, with one clear takeaway.”

Faceless means you do not need to appear on camera. Your video can still include illustrated characters, objects, narration and visual storytelling.

What is a faceless video?

A faceless video lets you tell a story without appearing on camera. It can use animated characters, diagrams, narration or scenes to carry the idea.

Make a series with ShortClipr

Choose a topic you can revisit, such as surprising history, everyday science or short mysteries. Write one focused brief per episode, choose a creative format and use the creation desk to explore the result.

A practical first brief

Explain why the moon is sometimes visible during the day. Make it friendly for beginners, use a vertical frame and end with one clear takeaway. Check the final explanation before publishing.
