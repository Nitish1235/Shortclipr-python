Our Copyright Commitment

ShortClipr is an AI-powered animated video studio. The platform generates declarative animations from user-supplied briefs using AI planning, text-to-speech narration, and a programmatic renderer. We do not host, distribute, or index third-party copyrighted films, music tracks, or images as part of the core product.

If you believe content published on shortclipr.com (such as showcase clips or guide images) infringes your copyright, please submit a notice using the process below.

Submitting a DMCA Takedown Notice

To file a valid notice under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (or the equivalent process under applicable law), send the following information to dmca@shortclipr.com:

  1. Your full legal name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  3. The specific URL(s) on shortclipr.com where the allegedly infringing material appears.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on the owner's behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

⚡ Send DMCA notices to: dmca@shortclipr.com — we aim to acknowledge within 2 business days.

What Happens Next

Upon receiving a valid notice we will:

  • Acknowledge receipt within 2 business days.
  • Remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing material promptly while we investigate.
  • Notify the account holder whose content was removed (if applicable).
  • Provide information about how to file a counter-notice if the removal was in error.

Counter-Notices

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice to dmca@shortclipr.com containing:

  1. Your full legal name, address, telephone, and email.
  2. Identification of the removed material and the URL where it previously appeared.
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your consent to the jurisdiction of your local federal district court (or equivalent).
  5. Your physical or electronic signature.

Upon receiving a valid counter-notice, we may restore the removed material after 10–14 business days unless the original complainant initiates legal action.

Repeat Infringers

ShortClipr has a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who are repeat copyright infringers.

Misuse of the DMCA Process

Filing a false DMCA notice is a serious matter that can result in legal liability. Please ensure your notice is filed in good faith and accurately describes the infringing material.
