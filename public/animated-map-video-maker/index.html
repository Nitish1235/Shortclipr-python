WORKFLOW & FIT

Make geography and history immediately visible.

Static maps overwhelm short-form viewers with dozens of tiny labels. Animated map shorts work by spotlighting one route or border change at a time—moving the viewer's eye from origin to destination while narration explains why that path mattered.

Worked brief example

“Create a 45-second 16:9 animated map short tracing how spices moved from the Maluku Islands across the Indian Ocean to Venice in the 1400s. Highlight three waypoints clearly, show maritime vs. overland legs in contrasting colors, and note that map proportions are stylized.”

Because geography and historical borders are sensitive to error, always supply verified place names and dates in your brief and double-check every label before publishing.

COMMON QUESTIONS

CLEAR ANSWERS
BEFORE YOU START.

Should animated map videos be 9:16 vertical or 16:9 widescreen?+

Use 16:9 widescreen for east–west trade routes and wide regional comparisons; use 9:16 vertical for YouTube Shorts/TikTok when zooming step-by-step along waypoints.

Are AI-generated map animations cartographically exact?+

Treat stylized AI map animations as visual storytelling aids rather than survey-grade cartography. Always verify borders, coastlines, and labels during review.

How many locations should I put in a 45-second map short?+

Stick to 3 to 5 clear waypoints so labels remain large and legible on phone screens.

Can I create fictional fantasy map journeys too?+

Yes—just clearly frame fictional worlds as fictional in your narration or caption.
