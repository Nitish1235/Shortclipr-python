LEGAL

PRIVACY
POLICY.

We believe you have the right to know exactly what data ShortClipr holds about you and why.

Last updated: 8 November 2026  ·  Effective: 8 November 2026

1. Who Controls Your Data

ShortClipr ("we", "us") is the data controller for personal information collected through shortclipr.com. Contact us at privacy@shortclipr.com for any privacy-related questions.

2. What We Collect

We do not collect payment card numbers, bank details, or government identification numbers. Payment is handled entirely by Dodo Payments.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing the service: Creating your account, running render jobs, storing output files, sending project-ready emails.
  • Billing: Tracking your subscription status and credit balance; processing renewals and cancellations via Dodo Payments.
  • Platform improvement: Analysing failure logs and render metrics to improve quality and reliability.
  • Communications: Sending transactional emails (project complete, payment events). We do not send unsolicited marketing email without consent.
  • Legal compliance: Retaining records as required by applicable law.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

  • Contract performance: Processing necessary to deliver the service you signed up for (account, renders, billing).
  • Legitimate interests: Security monitoring, fraud prevention, service reliability.
  • Consent: Analytics and advertising pixels (you can opt out via browser controls or our cookie settings).
  • Legal obligation: Records required by applicable law.

5. Data Sharing

We share your data only with the third parties necessary to operate ShortClipr:

  • Google Cloud Platform — database (Supabase/PostgreSQL) and file storage (Google Cloud Storage). Files are stored in private buckets, accessible only via signed time-limited URLs.
  • OpenAI — your project brief and scene data are sent to OpenAI to generate narration scripts and animation programs. OpenAI's API data usage policy applies.
  • Unreal Speech — narration text is sent to synthesise audio. No personal data beyond the narration script is transmitted.
  • Dodo Payments — your email address and subscription details for billing. Card data is handled solely by Dodo Payments.
  • Resend — your name and email address to send project-ready notifications.
  • Google, Meta, TikTok — anonymised conversion events (sign-up, subscription) for advertising analytics, subject to your consent.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

6. Output File Storage and Retention

Rendered video files are stored privately on Google Cloud Storage and accessible only to you via a time-limited signed URL. We retain output files for as long as your account remains active. You can delete a project at any time from the studio.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: Request your project data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent: Opt out of analytics pixels at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@shortclipr.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking

ShortClipr uses:

  • Essential cookies: JWT authentication token (stored in browser memory / localStorage). No consent required.
  • Analytics: Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel — set only after interaction or based on browser settings. These can be blocked using standard browser privacy controls or an ad blocker.

9. Data Security

We use HTTPS throughout the platform, database connections are encrypted in transit, output files are stored in private GCS buckets, and internal service calls are authenticated with rotating secrets. No system is perfectly secure; we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your Google account.

10. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active, then deleted within 90 days of account closure.
  • Project files: retained until you delete the project or close your account.
  • Billing records: retained for 7 years as required for financial record-keeping.
  • Analytics logs: retained in aggregated form; individual events purged after 26 months.

11. Children

ShortClipr is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at privacy@shortclipr.com and we will delete it promptly.

12. Changes to This Policy

We will post any material changes to this page and update the "Last updated" date above. Where changes significantly affect your rights, we will also notify you by email at least 7 days in advance.

13. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@shortclipr.com
