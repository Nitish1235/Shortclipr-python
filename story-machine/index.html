QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you create.

The practical details, without the fine print hunt.

Do I need to be on camera?+

No. ShortClipr is designed for faceless animated shorts made from your ideas.

Do I have to edit a timeline?+

No. You choose the brief and creative direction, then receive a finished video to watch and download.

Can I choose the voice and visual style?+

Yes. Choose from the supported voices and visual styles, or let AI choose the visual direction for your topic.

Which lengths and aspect ratios are available?+

Paid plans offer 15, 30, 45, and 60-second shorts in 9:16, 1:1, or 16:9. The free plan includes one 15-second short.
