# ShortClipr clip-type card art

Updated: 4 November 2026

## Purpose

Each creation-desk card should preview the visual language of a finished ShortClipr film. The artwork is not a decorative category icon or a generic cinematic background.

## Shared prompt direction

- Produce a professional animation keyframe in a wide landscape composition.
- Keep the primary subject in the center-right 55% of the frame.
- Keep the left 40% quieter, darker and lower-detail for interface copy.
- Use one instantly readable visual idea and a clear silhouette; the image must work at approximately 300 × 180 pixels.
- Match the production medium to the format rather than forcing one style across the library.
- Use a deep charcoal foundation with restrained ShortClipr accents: acid lime, dusty lavender, coral and electric cyan.
- Avoid words, letters, numbers, captions, logos, watermarks, UI, device mockups, poster layouts, decorative borders, excessive particles and generic glossy 3D rendering.

When producing contact sheets, use an exact 3 × 2 grid with six isolated equal panels, straight narrow charcoal gutters and no objects crossing panel boundaries.

## UI treatment

The image remains visible in every state. A directional dark overlay protects the title and description on the left. Hover increases saturation and scale slightly. Selection uses the lime border and lavender shadow rather than replacing the artwork with a flat fill.
