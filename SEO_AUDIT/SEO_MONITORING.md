# ShortClipr SEO Monitoring

## Search Console setup

Manual verification is required for:

- Domain/property ownership.
- Submitted sitemap status.
- Canonical selection.
- Mobile rendering.
- Page indexing.
- Video indexing where applicable.

No Search Console access or data is claimed by this document.

## Reports to monitor

### Performance

Track clicks, impressions, CTR, position, device, country, query, and page. Group results by Create, Explore, and Grow intent.

### Indexing

Review indexed, excluded, duplicate, alternate-canonical, crawled-not-indexed, and discovered-not-indexed pages.

### Sitemaps

Confirm the submitted sitemap contains only canonical indexable URLs and that submitted/indexed counts are reasonable.

### URL Inspection

Inspect the homepage, each priority product page, one guide, one legal page, and any page with an unexpected canonical or indexing state.

### Links

Monitor internal-link discovery and genuine external mentions. Do not pursue purchased or automated spam links.

### Manual actions and security

Review regularly, especially after launches, redirects, or content migrations.

## Product analytics

Track organic sessions and assisted conversions to:

- `/studio/create`
- completed prompt submissions
- completed video generations
- video plays on public showcase pages
- downloads where measurement is privacy-compliant

## Review cadence

- After every SEO deployment: run the local audit and crawl the changed routes.
- Weekly after launch: review indexing, crawl errors, and newly discovered queries.
- Monthly: review page/query performance, cannibalization, CTR, and assisted conversions.
- Quarterly: refresh claims and content only when product capability, intent, or factual guidance has materially changed.
