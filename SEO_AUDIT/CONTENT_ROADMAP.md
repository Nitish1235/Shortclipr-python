# ShortClipr Content Roadmap

## Phase 1 — establish the product identity

1. Rewrite homepage metadata and the above-the-fold explanation around “idea or prompt to animated short.”
2. Keep Story Machine visual branding and artwork.
3. Add a real generated-video showcase with prompt, category, duration, format, and CTA.
4. Make the text-to-animation and faceless-video pages distinct from the homepage.

## Phase 2 — improve existing commercial pages

For each core product page, add:

- A direct answer in the opening section.
- A realistic prompt example.
- The scene or script plan where useful.
- A real finished output when available.
- Supported duration, aspect ratio, voice, and style information.
- Who the page is for and when it is not the right tool.
- One relevant guide link and one creation CTA.
- Unique FAQs that answer the page’s actual intent.

Priority order:

1. `/text-to-animation/`
2. `/faceless-video-maker/`
3. `/animated-explainer-video-maker/`
4. `/educational-video-maker/`
5. `/video-quiz-maker/`
6. `/animated-story-video-maker/`
7. `/faceless-ai-shorts-generator/`

## Phase 3 — consolidate and strengthen guides

Classify every guide as keep, expand, merge, redirect, noindex, or remove after checking intent overlap and Search Console data. Prioritize original value over article count.

Recommended additions after the existing library is improved:

1. 50 Faceless YouTube Channel Ideas.
2. 30 Animated Video Ideas You Can Create With AI.
3. How to Make Animated Videos Without Animation Skills.
4. How to Turn Text Prompts Into Animated Videos.
5. Best AI Animated Video Generators.
6. How to Create Educational Shorts With AI.
7. How to Make Animated History Shorts.
8. How to Make Science Shorts for YouTube.
9. 100 AI Animation Prompts With Examples.
10. How to Create a Faceless YouTube Channel.
11. How to Make a 60-Second Animated Story.
12. AI Animation vs Traditional Animation.

Each article should include a worked example, a creator checklist, accurate limitations, and contextual internal links. Research every topic before publishing.

## Phase 4 — build the video ideas library

Improve `/video-ideas/` with search/filtering, useful descriptions, prompt examples, suggested duration, audience, related format, real output, and a creation CTA. Do not generate hundreds of thin category pages.

## Content quality rules

- Never present concept art as customer output.
- Never claim automatic publishing to third-party platforms unless supported.
- Never invent testimonials, metrics, rankings, integrations, or product capabilities.
- Show dates only when content was materially reviewed.
- Prefer one excellent page over multiple near-duplicates.
